The Weight Rule

    
The tongue sticks to whatever it hits, and the lighter side moves. The frog weighs 3. Turn on hitboxes to see every object's weight in the room.

    

      

        
Lighter: it comes to you

        
    
          
  • W0 flies are eaten and heal one heart
    • 
          
  • W1 pebbles go in the throat sac; X spits them back out
    • 
          
  • W1 dragonflies are worth three flies
    • 
          
  • W2 shell beetles flip over on the first lick, get eaten on the second
    • 
        

      

      

        
Level with you: you drag it

        
    
          
  • W2 lily pads slide across the water toward you, so you can build your own bridge

Heavier: you go to it

        
    
          
  • W∞ cattails and rings: the tongue latches on and you swing on your own; Jump lets go with a boost
    • 
          
  • W5 Mossback the turtle: tongue him to hop aboard, croak to call him over

Why it carries a whole game

        
One button and one number cover pickup, traversal, puzzles and combat. Later areas only need to change weights: a gulped stone makes the frog heavier (sinks, breaks planks, now drags things that used to drag it), a bubble makes it lighter. Bosses can be heavier or lighter by phase.
